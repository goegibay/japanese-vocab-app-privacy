Kotoba Forest（言葉森林）

Learn JLPT Japanese vocabulary by growing your own forest.

What it is

Kotoba Forest is a gamified Japanese vocabulary app for JLPT learners. You grow your own forest as you study — every word you learn and review makes your forest grow, turning daily vocabulary practice into a relaxing game you actually want to come back to.

The promise

Turn memorizing Japanese vocabulary into a daily habit you actually keep — through spaced repetition, JLPT-style mock tests, and a cozy "grow your forest" game loop that makes studying feel effortless.

Core features

  • JLPT N5–N1 vocabulary — thousands of words across every level
  • Spaced-repetition review (SRS) — plant → water → harvest; words move into long-term memory
  • JLPT mock tests + weak-point drills to target exactly what you keep getting wrong
  • Cute animal characters & mini-games that make daily review fun
  • Human-verified meanings and example sentences — curated by people, not AI-generated guesses
  • Offline-friendly; interface available in multiple languages

Who it's for

People learning Japanese — especially JLPT test-takers (N5–N1) and anime / Japan-culture-driven casual learners who want studying to feel like a game instead of a chore.

The vibe

Cozy, relaxing, cute, satisfying — a therapeutic little forest world. It's a highly visual experience that looks great in short-form video and slideshow content: watch trees grow, animals roam, and your progress bloom.
