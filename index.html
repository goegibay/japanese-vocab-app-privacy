隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026-07-28

English version

「言葉森林 Kotoba Forest」（以下稱「本 App」，Android 套件名稱及 iOS Bundle ID 均為 com.kotobaforest.app）由個人開發者開發與維運。本政策說明本 App 在您使用服務時，會蒐集哪些資訊、如何使用，以及您擁有的權利。

我們蒐集的資訊

  • 帳號資訊：首次使用時本 App 會建立匿名帳號（不需提供任何個人資訊）。當您選擇以 Email、Google 帳號或 Sign in with Apple 註冊／登入時，我們會儲存登入服務提供的帳號識別碼及 Email 地址（若您選擇「隱藏我的電子郵件」，可能是 Apple 提供的轉寄地址），用於帳號識別、登入驗證與跨裝置進度備份。
  • 學習進度資料：包含您的單字複習紀錄（間隔複習階段、下次複習時間）、連續學習天數、種子與語彙果數量、已擁有與放置的角色、森林狀態等，用於提供個人化的學習功能。
  • 購買資訊：若您訂閱 VIP 會員，付款由 Google Play 或 Apple App Store 處理；我們會接收購買憑證、商品方案、訂閱平台與有效期間，用於驗證及維護會員狀態，但不會取得您的信用卡號或完整付款帳戶資訊。
  • 匿名使用數據：為了改善產品，我們會記錄匿名的 App 互動事件（例如完成一次複習、開始一次測驗等操作類型與發生時間），並以帳號的假名化識別碼（pseudonymous ID）關聯。這些事件不包含任何個人身分資訊、輸入內容或裝置廣告 ID，僅用於彙總統計與功能改善分析。

本 App 不顯示廣告，不使用廣告 SDK，也不會蒐集裝置廣告 ID。

我們如何使用這些資訊

  • 提供、維護並改善 App 的核心功能（單字學習、間隔複習、森林養成、角色商店等）
  • 在您重新開啟 App 或更換裝置時恢復登入狀態與學習進度
  • 驗證 VIP 會員訂閱狀態

資料儲存與第三方服務

本 App 使用以下第三方服務來儲存與處理資料：

  • Supabase（資料庫與帳號驗證）：儲存您的帳號資訊與學習進度資料
  • Google（Google 登入、Google Play 應用程式內購買）：在您選用時處理登入或 Android VIP 訂閱付款
  • Apple（Sign in with Apple、App Store 應用程式內購買）：在您選用時處理登入或 iOS VIP 訂閱付款
  • 內容傳遞服務：傳遞預錄發音等靜態學習資產；不會向該服務傳送您的帳號資料或學習進度

部分學習資料亦會快取在您的裝置本機，以支援離線學習。我們不會將您的個人資料出售給第三方，亦不會用於與本 App 功能無關的行銷目的。

帳號與資料刪除

您可以隨時要求刪除帳號與相關伺服器端資料，包括 Email、學習進度、遊戲狀態與使用事件。依法令、稅務、防詐或商店交易爭議所必須保留的最少交易驗證紀錄，可能依必要期間保留；付款方式資料由 Google Play 或 Apple App Store 管理，不由本 App 保存。刪除方式有兩種：

  1. App 內申請：開啟 App → 「設定」分頁 → 點選最下方「刪除帳號」→ 依畫面指示送出刪除請求。
  2. Email 申請：寄信至 stevenhuang42195@gmail.com，主旨註明「刪除帳號請求」並附上您的註冊 Email。

我們會於收到請求後 7 個工作天內完成刪除。刪除後所有學習進度無法復原；刪除 App 帳號不會自動取消商店訂閱，VIP 訂閱請另行於 Google Play 或 Apple App Store 的「訂閱」頁面取消，避免持續扣款。

資料保留

您的帳號資訊與學習進度會持續保留，直到您依上述方式要求刪除帳號為止。

兒童隱私

本 App 並非專門針對兒童設計，亦不會刻意蒐集 13 歲以下兒童的個人資訊。

政策異動

我們可能不定期更新本政策。重大變更時會更新本頁面上方的「最後更新日期」。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

“Kotoba Forest” (the “App”; Android package and iOS bundle identifier com.kotobaforest.app) is developed and operated by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: The App creates an anonymous account on first use without asking for personal information. If you choose email, Google, or Sign in with Apple, we store the account identifier and email address supplied by that provider. An Apple private relay address may be used when you choose Hide My Email.
  • Learning progress: This includes vocabulary review history, spaced-repetition stage and due time, learning streak, in-app seed and fruit balances, owned and placed characters, and forest state.
  • Purchase information: Google Play or the Apple App Store processes VIP subscription payments. We receive a purchase credential, product tier, purchase platform, and entitlement period to verify and maintain VIP access. We do not receive or store your credit card number or full payment account details.
  • Pseudonymous usage data: We record limited in-app interaction events, such as starting a quiz or completing a review, together with the event time and a pseudonymous account identifier. These events do not contain free-form input, advertising identifiers, or direct identity details and are used for aggregate analytics and product improvement.

The App has no advertising, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not collect an advertising identifier.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve vocabulary learning, spaced review, forest, and character features
  • Restore your sign-in state and progress when you reopen the App or change devices
  • Verify and maintain VIP subscription access

Storage and Service Providers

  • Supabase: database storage and account authentication
  • Google: optional Google sign-in and Google Play purchases on Android
  • Apple: optional Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases on iOS
  • Content delivery services: delivery of static learning assets such as prerecorded pronunciation; account details and learning progress are not sent to this service

Some learning data is cached locally for offline use. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for marketing unrelated to the App.

Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated server data, including email, learning progress, game state, and usage events. Minimum transaction-verification records may be retained where required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or store dispute purposes. Payment methods remain with Google Play or Apple and are not stored by the App.

  1. In the App: open Settings → Delete Account and follow the on-screen instructions.
  2. By email: send a message to stevenhuang42195@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request” and include your registered email address.

We complete deletion within seven business days. Deleted progress cannot be restored. Deleting your Kotoba Forest account does not automatically cancel a store subscription; cancel VIP separately in Google Play or the Apple App Store to avoid future charges.

Retention

Account information and learning progress are retained until you request account deletion as described above.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the updated date at the top of this page.
